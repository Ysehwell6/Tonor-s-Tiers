What is Tonor's Tiers?

*Unfiltered, objective AI mechanical audits for Minecraft PvP.*

*Tonor's Tiers eliminates bias from community placement reviews. By leveraging advanced AI models, match clips are inspected down to the frame—evaluating crosshair placement, spacing, combo rhythm, and resource management with zero sugarcoating.*

System Submission Guide

*Step-by-step instructions on how to get tested and officially ranked.*

*Step 1: Find a friend or someone you know to consent to duel you in an FT5 match in any of our supported MCTiers game modes.*

*Step 2: Record you and your opponent fighting. When the match concludes, end the recording.*

*Step 3: Start a fresh screen-recording to capture you submitting that fight clip to the AI. Do not end that screen record until the AI finishes responding and you scroll down to show the entire response report.*

*Step 4: Upload both your fight clip and screen-recording to MediaFire, then join the Tonor's Tiers Discord server.*

*Step 5: Navigate to the #request-an-analyst channel and tag @analyst.*

*Once you are officially verified and marked safe by the analyst, your MCTiers rankings sync live via public API!*
