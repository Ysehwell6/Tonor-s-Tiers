What is Tonor's Tiers?

*Unfiltered, objective AI mechanical audits for Minecraft PvP.*

*Tonor's Tiers eliminates bias from community placement reviews. By leveraging Google's advanced multimodal AI models, match clips are inspected down to the frame—evaluating crosshair placement, spacing, combo rhythm, and resource management with zero sugarcoating.*

*The system utilizes fine-tuned system instructions to map visual player input directly to high-tier combat benchmarks. Every audit breaks down defensive weaknesses and offensive openings with precise, raw metrics.*

System Submission Guide

*Step-by-step instructions on how to get tested and officially ranked.*

*You NEED a Google Gemini API key to operate the local auditing software.*

*Step 1: Find a friend or someone you know to consent to duel you in an FT5 match in any of our supported game modes: Mace, Spear-Mace, UHC, Sword, Axe, Dia Pot, Neth Pot, Dia SMP, Neth SMP.*

*Step 2: Record you and your opponent fighting. When the match concludes, end the recording.*

*Step 3: Start a fresh screen-recording to capture you submitting that fight clip to the AI. Do not end that screen record until the AI finishes responding and you scroll down to show the entire response report.*

*Step 4: Upload both your fight clip and screen-recording to MediaFire, then join the Tonor's Tiers Discord server at discord.gg/eusN8BYAg5.*

*Step 5: Navigate to the #request-an-analyst channel and tag @analyst. Any other use of @ tagging is strictly prohibited and will result in a permanent ban.*

*Step 6: When an analyst is ready, they will open a private ticket tab where you will send both MediaFire clip links.*

*Step 7: The analyst will thoroughly inspect both video files to ensure zero editing and verify that the AI gave a fair, unaltered rating.*

*Outcomes of the Audit:
1. Your role is found invalid if the AI made an evaluation mistake; the analyst will then adjust your role up or down.
2. If you are caught tampering with or modifying either video file, you WILL be permanently banned.*

*Once you are officially verified and marked safe by the analyst, you will be added directly to the Leaderboard tab!*
